# Community code of conduct

SubTranslateAI should be a welcoming project for users and contributors of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Accept correction and give actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy, copyright and responsible security disclosure.
- Help keep support spaces useful to newcomers.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, sexualized attention and deliberate disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Reports can be made privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) when public discussion would expose sensitive information.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact and repeated behavior. Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.
