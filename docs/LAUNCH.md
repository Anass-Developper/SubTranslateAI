# Honest launch plan

The goal is to find real subtitle users and contributors, not to manufacture stars. Publish measurable demos, respond quickly to early reports and keep setup friction low.

## Assets to prepare

- a 30–45 second screen recording showing installation, the extension popup and bilingual playback;
- one screenshot of the desktop control panel;
- one before/after subtitle example using original or freely licensed text;
- a short table with tested GPU, first-translation latency and steady-state latency;
- links to the release, privacy policy and issue tracker.

## English announcement

> I built SubTranslateAI, an open-source Windows app and Chrome/Edge extension for local French/Chinese dual subtitles. It translates existing FR, ZH or EN subtitle tracks with Hy-MT2 through Ollama, keeps the text on your PC, and needs no API key or subscription. The project is an early beta, so feedback on setup, synchronization and streaming-site compatibility is especially useful. Source and download: https://github.com/Anass-Developper/SubTranslateAI

## Good launch channels

- GitHub Discussions and relevant `awesome-*` lists;
- communities about language learning, Chinese/French learners and local AI;
- Ollama and open-source translation communities;
- a short demo on Reddit, X, LinkedIn or YouTube where self-promotion is allowed;
- direct invitations to a small group of genuine testers with different GPUs.

Always read a community's self-promotion rules. Do not buy stars, trade stars or mass-post identical messages: those users will not become active contributors and artificial engagement can damage trust.

## Metrics worth tracking

- successful first installations;
- weekly active testers who voluntarily report back;
- release downloads;
- setup completion and common failure reasons from user-submitted diagnostics;
- median translation latency by hardware;
- confirmed working websites per release;
- issues resolved and first-time contributors retained.
